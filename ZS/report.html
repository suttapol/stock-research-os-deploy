Revenue has compounded from $1.1B (FY22) to $2.7B (FY25), a 3-year CAGR of ~35%, now growing at 25% with re-acceleration signals. The company is pre-GAAP profitability, investing aggressively in S&M and R&D (combined ~54% of revenue), and operating in a market where TAM is still being created by regulatory and architectural change. This is the classic Fast Grower profile: high growth, heavy investment, cash flow positive before GAAP.

Implication: Lynch's Fast Grower rule is: "find companies growing 20–25%, and if the story's intact, stay with it." The critical questions are (1) PEG ratio — forward PE ~40x on non-GAAP EPS growing ~12-15% QoQ extrapolated = PEG roughly 2.5-3x (not cheap by Lynch standards); (2) how many years of 25%+ growth remain before maturity compression hits 15%; and (3) whether the company can survive a valuation de-rating if growth stumbles. Standard DCF overstates value if growth assumptions bake in terminal values prematurely. ---
