Peter Lynch Classification Stalwart 🏛️

Apple's 3-year revenue CAGR (FY2022–FY2025) was ~1.8% — historically Slow Grower territory. The iPhone 17 + Apple Intelligence inflection has pushed current YoY growth to 15–17%, temporarily resembling a Fast Grower. However, at $4.57T market cap and $451B TTM revenue with 166,000 employees, it lacks the "small-to-mid size" prerequisite of a genuine Fast Grower. Services' recurring annuity base supports a mild premium to typical Stalwarts.

Implication: Classic Stalwart risk is overpaying for a temporary supercycle. Standard DCF risks overweighting the current 16% growth rate. Evaluation should anchor on: (1) normalized post-cycle growth rate (likely 8–10%), (2) Services as a recurring, high-margin annuity justifying a persistent premium, and (3) buyback yield (~2%) as an EPS floor. Fair-cycle valuation at 28–30x forward P/E vs. current 32.4x implies ~8–12% downside if the cycle rolls before consensus expects. ---
