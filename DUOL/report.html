Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

Revenue grew 44.9% (Q1 2024), 41.5% (Q2 2025), 41.1% (Q3 2025) — classic Fast Grower territory. The business is mid-sized ($1.04B revenue in FY2025), still scaling with subscription economics, minimal physical assets, and no dividend. However, the deceleration to 18% in Q2 2026 and guided 11% in Q3 2026 signals the business is approaching the transition zone between Fast Grower and Stalwart.

Implication: Fast Grower → P/E alone is misleading; the relevant question is how many more years of 20%+ growth remain. At the current trajectory, the growth premium embedded in the valuation (P/S 5.9x, EV/EBITDA 30.5x) requires a re-acceleration to 20%+ by 2027 to justify the multiple. If growth settles at 10–15%, DUOL should be repriced as a Stalwart, implying further multiple compression. PEG at current 18% growth and forward P/E of ~18.9x yields a PEG of ~1.05x — fair but not cheap if growth continues decelerating. ---
