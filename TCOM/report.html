Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

Revenue growth has been 15–21% YoY for 8 consecutive quarters — well above Stalwart territory (10–12%). However, the base effect is now normalizing (post-reopening surge ended in FY2023) and the deceleration from 29% → 17% over 5 quarters suggests TCOM is transitioning from classic Fast Grower dynamics toward a large-cap travel compounder. The company is capital-light (minimal CapEx, ~¥6.5B PP&E on ¥263B of assets), has a dominant market position, and consistent gross margins of 79–82%.

Implication: For Fast Grower evaluation, P/E alone misleads — the stock's apparent cheapness (6.7x P/E) is partially an artifact of non-recurring investment income inflating the trailing earnings base. The correct question is: how many years can TCOM sustain 15%+ revenue growth before settling into 8–10% growth? If the runway is 3–5 more years (driven by international expansion + outbound recovery), the current valuation is compelling. If China OTA growth structurally slows to 8–10% within 2 years, current multiples are less attractive. **PEG** (based on operating earnings growth ~10–15%) is the right lens, not headline P/E. ---
