Conviction
Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

Revenue is expanding at 22–33% YoY across the last five quarters and re-accelerating — squarely in Fast Grower territory. Despite the $1.55T market cap and the initiation of a dividend (Stalwart signals), the current growth velocity is driven by AI-led ad efficiency and multiple under-monetized surfaces (WhatsApp Business, Meta AI), none of which are at maturity. Meta's gross margins (81%+) and operating margins (40%+) resemble a software business, not a cyclical advertiser. Balance sheet leverage has risen but operating cash flow ($124B TTM) dwarfs interest obligations. Cyclicality is low: digital advertising held up through the 2022 macro tightening, and Meta's first-party data advantages are insulated from macro shifts better than brand advertisers.

Implication: For a Fast Grower, P/E alone is misleading. Forward P/E of 17x on 33% revenue growth implies a PEG of ~0.5x — unusually inexpensive for this quality of compounder. The priority analytical questions are: (1) **Runway length** — how many years can 20%+ growth sustain before WhatsApp, Meta AI, and RL approach saturation? (2) **CapEx payback** — is the $58.9B FY2025 infrastructure spend shortening or lengthening the runway? The Lynch lens says: don't sell a Fast Grower cheaply just because it is large; raise conviction ceiling only when growth decelerates below ~20% for two consecutive quarters with no new surface to reignite it. Current data does not yet show that deceleration. ---
