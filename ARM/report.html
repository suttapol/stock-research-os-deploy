Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

ARM is generating 20–35% YoY revenue growth driven by a secular shift toward higher-royalty AI/data center silicon and architecture version migration (v8→v9). This is not cyclical recovery — it is structural expansion of TAM as compute increasingly moves onto ARM-licensed silicon in new end-markets (cloud AI, automotive, IoT) where ARM previously had minimal royalty exposure. The company commands 97.5% gross margins and is plowing 50–60% of revenue back into R&D to extend its moat, the classic growth-phase investment pattern. Annual revenue has grown from $2,679M (FY2023) to $4,920M (FY2026), a 22–24% CAGR.

Implication: In a Fast Grower framework, the central risk is not margin compression or cyclical earnings volatility — it is **growth rate deceleration**. The thesis lives or dies on whether 20%+ revenue CAGR is sustainable. The primary analytical focus must therefore be on new licensee signings, royalty per unit trends, and data center royalty ramp — not on quarter-to-quarter EPS beats. Conviction ceiling is constrained by the extreme valuation (105x forward PE), which implies much of the growth is already priced in for years. Position sizing must reflect that even a fundamentally correct long-term thesis could produce a 40%+ drawdown if growth temporarily dips below the embedded expectation. ---
