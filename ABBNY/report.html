ABB is a 140-year-old, $35B+ revenue industrial conglomerate with 111,900 employees. Annual revenue growth averaged ~7% from FY2020–FY2023 and has recently accelerated to ~14% driven by energy transition. This is not organic structural Fast Grower expansion — it reflects a macro tailwind bolting onto a mature platform. Business cyclicality (industrial automation demand correlates with global capex cycles) and extreme size make "Fast Grower" inappropriate despite the current growth rate. The Stalwart framing is reinforced by consistent dividends ($1.7B/year), multi-decade incumbency, and limited geographic concentration risk.

Implication: For a Stalwart, valuation discipline is paramount — at 27.3x forward P/E, ABB must sustain 12%+ earnings growth to justify the multiple. The appropriate question is not "will they dominate a new market?" but "will growth hold above the implied 10–12% plateau, and for how long?" Entry price matters significantly; buying at 36x TTM earnings leaves little room for disappointment. ---
