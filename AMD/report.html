Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

AMD has delivered 31–38% YoY revenue growth for 5 consecutive quarters on a ~$37.5B TTM base, with earnings growing 91% YoY (yfinance). Gross margins have expanded from 44.9% (FY2022) to 52.8% (Q1 2026) — a company still in its operating leverage build-up phase, not a mature steady-state. The AI infrastructure buildout is a secular demand shift, not a commodity inventory cycle, distinguishing AMD from a Cyclical classification despite the inherent cyclicality of semiconductors.

Implication: For a Fast Grower, TTM P/E (173x) is meaningless; the operative figure is forward P/E (40x). The correct analytical lens is PEG: at ~38% earnings growth, PEG ≈ 40/38 ≈ 1.05 — acceptable for a Fast Grower with expanding margins. Conviction ceiling is determined by how many years AMD can sustain 30%+ growth before the law of large numbers, NVIDIA competition, or a hyperscaler AI capex cycle forces deceleration. Standard DCF models that assume mean-reverting margins will systematically undervalue AMD if the Data Center thesis plays out; equally, they will correctly flag the downside if growth decelerates to 10–15% while the multiple remains elevated. ---
