Peter Lynch Classification Stalwart 🏛️

Netflix generated 13–18% revenue growth over the last six quarters on a $45B+ base, well above GDP but below the 20–25% Fast Grower threshold — and the trajectory is decelerating (+13.4% in Q2 2026 vs. +17.6% two quarters prior). It is a large, well-known, globally recognized franchise with a mature balance sheet, steady buybacks, and expanding margins — classic Stalwart traits. The remaining Fast-Grower characteristics (aggressive margin expansion, ad-tier/live optionality) are real but priced in.

Implication: Valuation matters a lot for Stalwarts. At 21x P/E and 20x EV/EBITDA for a business decelerating from +17% to +13% growth, there is limited multiple-expansion upside — returns will come primarily from earnings growth + buybacks (mid-teens EPS growth is a plausible base case), not re-rating. Downside if the growth story breaks (e.g., ad tier disappoints, gross margin resets) is a re-rate to mid-teens P/E — call it 25–30% multiple compression. Conviction ceiling for a Stalwart at this multiple is moderate, not high — this is a "buy on drawdowns" name rather than a "load-the-truck at any price" name. ---
