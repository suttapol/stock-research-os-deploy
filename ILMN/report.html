Peter Lynch Classification Turnaround ↩️

Illumina's revenue contracted for four consecutive years (FY2022–FY2025), falling from $4,583M to $4,343M, largely due to the GRAIL acquisition disaster (~$6B loss on consolidation) that distracted management, diluted margins, and destroyed capital. The company has now divested GRAIL, reset cost structure, and is executing a recovery with operating margins expanding from -1.4% (FY2023) to 20.5% (FY2025). This is the defining signature of a Turnaround — a specific recovery catalyst (GRAIL exit) driving fundamental inflection.

Implication: As a Turnaround, the standard DCF or P/E framework requires careful calibration — trailing earnings are messy (lumpy GAAP from GRAIL-related gains/losses), and the "normalization" of earnings is the central question. The correct analytical lens is: (a) Is the recovery catalyst fully executed? (Answer: yes, GRAIL is gone.) (b) Can normalized FCF and margins be sustained? (Answer: FY2025 FCF of $931M and 20%+ op margins suggest yes.) (c) How much of the recovery is already priced in? (35.6x trailing P/E says quite a lot.) Conviction ceiling for a Turnaround is typically capped once recovery is priced; the bull case depends on whether this transitions into a Stalwart at 10%+ annual growth. ---
