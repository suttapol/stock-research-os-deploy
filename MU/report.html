Conviction
Peter Lynch Classification Cyclical 🔄

Memory semiconductors follow textbook boom-bust cycles. Micron's own history is the clearest evidence: FY2022 ($30.8B, +45% GM) → FY2023 ($15.5B, -9% GM) → FY2024 ($25.1B, +22% GM) → FY2025 ($37.4B, +40% GM) → FY2026 on track for $100B+ annualized. The current 346% YoY revenue growth and 84.6% gross margins look like a Fast Grower, but this is price/mix driven rather than unit-volume driven — a classic cyclical peak signal. Revenue volatility of ±50% within two years is definitionally cyclical, not compounding growth.

Implication: Standard DCF applied to peak-cycle earnings dramatically overstates intrinsic value for cyclical companies. Lynch's framework advises buying cyclicals when P/E is high (the trough, when losses are reported) and selling when P/E is low (the peak). At 8.97x forward PE on $100B+ annualized revenue, the market is applying a cyclical discount — the central analytical question is: how much of the 84.6% gross margin is structural (HBM pricing power, product mix) versus cyclical (supply constraint)? That ratio determines whether 9x forward PE is an opportunity or a value trap. ---
