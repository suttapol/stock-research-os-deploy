Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

Revenue has grown 14.9–18.4% YoY over the past three fiscal years, re-accelerating in the most recent four quarters. Earnings growth of 23.4% TTM (per yfinance) outpaces revenue growth, confirming margin expansion alongside topline gains. While Microsoft's $2.9T market cap is far beyond Lynch's typical small-to-mid cap Fast Grower, the business is being fundamentally repriced by a new product cycle (AI/Azure) that is extending the growth runway — the defining characteristic of the category.

Implication: Fast Grower framing means P/E alone is misleading — the PEG ratio matters more. At 20x forward P/E and 23% earnings growth, PEG ≈ 0.87x, which is actually undemanding. The key Lynch question is: **how many years can this growth rate be sustained before the law of large numbers forces deceleration?** Azure still represents a minority of global enterprise IT spend, suggesting a 5–7 year runway is plausible. Conviction ceiling: medium-high, with the caveat that at $2.9T, every 1% of incremental share requires capturing an enormous absolute dollar amount of new spend. ---
