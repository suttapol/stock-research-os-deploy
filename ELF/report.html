Peter Lynch Classification Fast Grower 🚀

ELF ran at a 43% revenue CAGR from FY2022–FY2026 — classic Fast Grower territory. But FY2027 guidance of 12–14% YoY, the step-up to $1.6B revenue, broad retail distribution maturity in US mass-market, and a $909M debt-load from acquisition all signal that the hypergrowth phase is ending. Gross margin stability (71%) and $190M FCF generation are hallmarks of a maturing Stalwart, not a scrappy grower.

Implication: For Stalwarts, the valuation ceiling is tighter — paying > 25× forward earnings requires conviction in sustained 12–15% growth AND margin recovery. At the current 15.7× forward PE, ELF is priced as a lower-quality Stalwart, which creates upside if management delivers rhode integration and restores 12%+ operating margins. The key risk is that the market never re-rates it back toward Fast Grower multiples (30–40×) — so upside is probably 1.5–2× over 3 years, not 5×. Conviction ceiling is moderate. ---
