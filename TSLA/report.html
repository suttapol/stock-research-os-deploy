Tesla's core Automotive revenue declined from $82.4B (FY2023) to $69.5B (FY2025). EPS fell from $4.30 → $1.08 over two years. Operating margin compressed from 9.2% to 4.6%. The company is now betting on specific, defined catalysts — Cybercab commercial production, Robotaxi in 7 metros, Energy scale-up, FSD monetization — to reverse the P&L deterioration. This is textbook Turnaround: beaten-down earnings trajectory with a concrete catalyst thesis driving the recovery story.

Implication: For Turnarounds, Lynch says "track the catalyst, not the story." Standard DCF or trailing P/E is misleading here — the 288× P/E embeds optionality that may or may not materialize. The correct analytical frame: Is Cybercab production growing month-over-month? Are Robotaxi ride counts and revenue appearing on the income statement? Are Energy gross margins expanding? These milestones are the conviction anchors, not the multiple. Exit if catalysts miss schedule — do not rationalize with narrative. ---
