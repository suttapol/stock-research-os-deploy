Ciena's revenue directly tracks carrier CapEx cycles — FY2024 posted -8.5% annual decline as carriers paused, then rebounded 30–40% YoY as spending resumed. This boom-bust pattern (inventory overbuild in FY2023, destocking in FY2024, re-acceleration in FY2025–2026) is textbook cyclical behavior. Revenue CAGR FY2022–FY2025 was only ~10% — consistent with a cyclical, not a structural fast grower. The AI/hyperscaler tailwind is real but layered on top of, not replacing, the underlying cyclical structure.

Implication: Standard trailing P/E (126x) is misleading — it reflects trough earnings, not mid-cycle earnings power. The more useful valuation anchor is mid-cycle EPS of ~$5–6/share (based on current trajectory and historical operating leverage). At $378, that implies 60–75x mid-cycle P/E — still demanding. Cyclical discipline: watch for the *next* inventory build in carrier land as the signal that the cycle is peaking again, not accelerating fundamentals. ---
