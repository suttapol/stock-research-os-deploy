iQIYI was a profitable, high-gross-margin business in FY2023 (gross margin 28.3%, operating income RMB 3,243M, net income RMB 1,925M). Since then, it has undergone a significant deterioration across all financial dimensions — declining revenue for 3 consecutive years, gross margin collapse of 1,240 bps, and operating cash flow falling from RMB 3,352M to RMB 106M. The business has not yet found its floor, and the current thesis depends on a specific recovery catalyst: whether AI-driven cost reduction and overseas expansion can arrest the margin and revenue declines before cash is exhausted.

Implication: As a Turnaround, the standard valuation framework (P/E, DCF on forward earnings) is unreliable because earnings are negative and the path to recovery is uncertain. The correct frame is: (1) Does a credible recovery catalyst exist? (2) Does the balance sheet provide enough runway to reach it? (3) What does the stock price if the turnaround succeeds vs. fails? At $1.35/share and $1.3B market cap, the market is pricing close to zero recovery probability — which creates asymmetric upside IF the catalyst materializes, but also means the stock is not obviously "cheap" if the structural decline continues. ---
